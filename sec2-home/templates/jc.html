JC Admission Guide

What JC cut-off points mean, how the score is worked out, how competitive each JC was in 2026, and what changes before your turn to apply in 2029.

What a cut-off point is

Lower is better

The cut-off is the score of the last student admitted to a JC. Students with that score or better had a good chance of getting in. Cut-offs move a little every year with demand.

Science vs Arts

Each JC has separate cut-offs for its Science and Arts streams. Science is usually a little harder to get into. A "–" means that stream isn't offered through the JAE.

Net score

Published cut-offs are net scores: the raw (gross) score minus bonus points. A cut-off of 3 usually means a raw score of about 6–7 with 3–4 bonus points.

CLEP-JC bonus

Students who apply for the Chinese Language Elective Programme (CLEP) at a JC through the JAE get 2 extra bonus points for admission to that JC. These are added on top of the usual maximum, so the total can reach 6 (or 5 from 2028).

CLEP is for students strong in Chinese language and culture. Only some JCs offer it, so check each JC's website, and ask the school about selection.

3. Example

Worked exampleGrades A1, A1, A2, A1, A2, B3 → gross score 1+1+2+1+2+3 = 10. With an Excellent CCA (2) and Higher Chinese passed (2), the bonus is 4. Net score = 10 − 4 = 6, which met the 2026 Science cut-off for Dunman High, National, St Joseph's and Victoria.

What to do in Sec 2

Sec 3 subject choice

The option exercise (22–26 Oct) decides which subjects can count. L1R4 needs one humanities and one maths or science, plus one more of either. Keep strong maths and science options open.

CCA record

A sustained CCA with participation, leadership and achievement can give 1–2 bonus points.

Higher Mother Tongue

Passing English and Higher Mother Tongue (A1–C6) gives 2 bonus points, and Higher MT can count as L1. Strong Higher Chinese also opens the door to CLEP-JC, worth 2 more points at the JCs that offer it.

Watch out: the 2028 rules above come from a tuition centre's summary of MOE's announcement. Check the details with the school or MOE before the option exercise.
